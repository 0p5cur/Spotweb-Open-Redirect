Attack Flow

  1. Attacker creates phishing page with malicious login form
  2. Form submits to real Spotweb with loginform[http_referer]=evil.com
  3. Victim enters valid credentials
  4. After successful login, victim is redirected to attacker's site
  5. Attacker harvests session or performs further attacks
